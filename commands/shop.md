# shop

buy with `/buy`, activate consumables with `/use`, check what you own with `/inventory`. prices are in coins. earn them in the [economy](economy.md).

items are not consumed automatically. a consumable is only used up when you activate it with `/use`.

---

## consumables

activate with `/use`.

| item | price | effect |
|---|---|---|
| padlock | 800 | shields you for 12 hours. blocks the next `/rob` against you. |
| lockpick | 500 | use it before a `/rob`. your next robbery works 65% of the time instead of 40%. |
| coffee | 600 | resets your `/work`, `/beg`, `/mine` and `/fish` cooldowns. |
| lucky clover | 1,500 | your next `/crime` or `/slut` is 20% less likely to fail. |

---

## tools

permanent. never used up.

| item | price | effect |
|---|---|---|
| iron pickaxe | 3,000 | `/mine` pays 25% more. |
| fishing rod | 2,000 | `/fish` pays 25% more. |
